# Energy Management

# Question 1: What are the activities you do that make you relax?

Taking a long walk and doing exercise. These activities help me avoid overthinking and keep my mind away from thoughts that cause me stress.



# Question 2: When do you find yourself getting into the Stress quadrant?

I find myself getting into the Stress quadrant when I overthink situations, worry too much about the future, or put too much pressure on myself. I also feel stressed when things don’t go as planned or when I have too many things to deal with at the same time.


# Question 2: How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?

I understand that I am in the Excitement quadrant when everything goes as planned. I feel happy, motivated, and positive about what I am doing.

# Question 4: Sleep Is Your Superpower

- Sleep is very important for our body and brain.
- Good sleep helps us remember and learn things better.
- It helps improve our focus, creativity, and decision-making.
- During sleep, our brain processes the things we learned during the day.
- Lack of sleep can make us less focused and affect our mood.
- Getting enough sleep regularly helps keep our body and mind healthy.

# Question 5: What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

- I can try to sleep and wake up at the same time every day.
- I can avoid using my phone just before going to bed.
- I can exercise regularly during the day.
- I can make sure that I get enough hours of sleep every night.

# Question 6: Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise

- Exercise makes me feel more energetic and improves my mood.
- Even one workout can help improve focus and attention.
- Exercise helps the brain release chemicals that make us feel happier.
- Regular exercise can improve our memory and learning ability.
- Exercise helps important parts of the brain, like the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex.
- It can help protect the brain from problems related to ageing.
- Exercise can help reduce stress and improve our overall mental health.
- We don't always need a gym; activities like walking, running, or climbing stairs can also be helpful.

# Question 7: What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

- I can start by walking for at least 30 minutes a few times a week.
- I can take the stairs instead of using the lift.
- I can include some simple exercise in my daily routine.
- I can try activities that I enjoy, so exercising doesn't feel like a burden.
- I can exercise 3–4 times a week and slowly make it a habit.
- I can go for a walk whenever I feel stressed or need a break.
- I can try different activities like running, cycling, dancing, or playing a sport.
- I can start with small steps instead of trying to do too much at once.
