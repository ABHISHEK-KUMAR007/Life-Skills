# Question 1

## What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. **Focus on the topic and speaker**
   - Focus on what the speaker is saying instead of getting distracted by your own thoughts.

2. **Do not interrupt**
   - Try not to interrupt the other person while they are speaking. Let them finish and then respond.

3. **Use door openers**
   - Encourage the speaker to continue by using phrases such as: “Tell me more.,  “Go ahead.", “I’m listening.” “That sounds great.”

4. **Show that you are listening through body language**
   - Use appropriate body language to show that you are paying attention.

5. **Take notes from the conversation**
   - Note down important points from the conversation.

6. **Paraphrase what the other person has said**
   - Repeat the main idea in your own words to make sure you have understood correctly and that both of you are on the same page.



# Question 2

## According to Fisher's Model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

- **Listen carefully to the speaker** and understand what they are trying to communicate.
- **Reflect the speaker’s body language** to show that you are engaged in the conversation.
- **Identify and acknowledge emotions** behind the speaker’s words.
- **Paraphrase/Summarize the message** in your own words to confirm your understanding.
- **Verify your understanding** by checking with the speaker if you understood their message correctly.
- **Ask questions for clarification** when something is unclear instead of assuming.
- **Make sure both people are on the same page** about what was discussed.
