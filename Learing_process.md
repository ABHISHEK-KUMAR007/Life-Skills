# Question 1
## What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.


The Feynman Technique is a method of learning where you learn by explaining a concept in an easy and simple way.

The reason is that if you cannot explain something in a simple way, you do not understand it well enough.

# Question 2
## In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

I find the story of Dalí quite interesting. Dalí was one of the most famous surrealist painters of the 20th century. When he got stuck while solving a problem related to his painting, he would sit down and relax in a chair, holding some keys in his hand and letting his brain noodle away.

When he fell asleep, the keys would fall from his hand, and the clatter would wake him up. He would then take the ideas he had discovered in the diffuse mode and bring them into the focused mode, where he could use them to refine his inventions and paintings.


# Question 3
## What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
Active mode is when your brain is actively thinking and trying to understand something.

Diffuse mode is when you keep your mind relaxed, letting your brain noodle away, and suddenly you realize the solution.
