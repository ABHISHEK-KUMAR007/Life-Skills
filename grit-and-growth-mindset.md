# Question 2
## Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.
Mindset plays an important role in a person’s growth. There are two ways to think about learning: **a fixed mindset** and **a growth mindset**.

A **fixed mindset** means believing that you do not have control over your abilities and that your skills cannot be changed or improved. On the other hand, a **growth mindset** means believing that you can develop your abilities through effort, learning, and practice. A growth mindset encourages people to be more innovative and open to learning.

There are two main characteristics that distinguish a growth mindset from a fixed mindset:

1. **Belief in growth:** People with a growth mindset believe that skills can be developed and that they can learn and improve over time. In contrast, people with a fixed mindset believe that certain abilities cannot be learned or changed.

2. **Focus:** People with a fixed mindset tend to focus on proving their performance and achieving immediate outcomes. People with a growth mindset focus more on the learning process, improving their skills, and becoming better over time.
